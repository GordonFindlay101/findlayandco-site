Farm Biosecurity

A dairy biosecurity plan, built online and kept with the farm gate

We have built an online course that walks a dairy farmer through writing a biosecurity plan for their own farm, and joined it to Farm SiteKey, our sign in for farm gates. This is what it does and the two ways to get it.

Gordon Findlay • Published 19 November 2026

In 2018 I wrote a Kellogg Rural Leadership Programme report on on-farm biosecurity planning, based on a survey of 49 New Zealand farmers. It recommended a digital solution and a whole-of-sector approach. The dairy course is the first one we have built.

What the course does

DairyNZ's Biosecurity Farm Plan works in three steps: look at your farm map, assess the risk pathways, and put a plan in place to manage the risks. The course follows the same order, one step to a page of the plan:

  1. Your property: who you are, what you produce, and who wrote the plan.
  2. Your map: gates, parking and the wash bay.
  3. What arrives and what leaves: stock, feed, gear and the jobs that move them.
  4. How things get in and out, for each of those.
  5. Rating the risks, and choosing what to sort out first.
  6. Your goals, one for each risk you put first.
  7. Who does what, including what you have agreed with contractors and other people who do not work for you.
  8. Four weeks later: what you actually saw, and what you changed. That becomes version 2.

The finished plan is emailed to the farmer as a PDF. When something changes on the farm, they change that step and press Update my plan, and the next version is emailed to them.

Why it is joined to the gate

DairyNZ recommends biosecurity signs at the entrance with your contact details on them (DairyNZ). Farm SiteKey prints a QR sign for each gate with the farm's hazards, site rules, contacts and Emergency 111. Visitors, contractors and staff scan it with their phone, tick the farm's gate questions and sign in. Lines from the plan can become gate questions. Contractors say where on the farm they will work and what they are doing, and that is kept with their sign in. The plan in Farm SiteKey and the plan in the course are the same plan.

Common questions

Is the plan in Farm SiteKey the same as the plan course you buy?

Yes. It is the same course and the same plan. With Farm SiteKey the farmer is enrolled when we approve the farm.

Can a farmer who already bought the plan move to Farm SiteKey?

Yes. The plan they have already written comes across into Farm SiteKey, and we credit what they paid for the plan against their Farm SiteKey account.

What happens after five months of Farm SiteKey?

Farm SiteKey carries on at $30 a month plus GST until the farm cancels.
