How should a New Zealand school sign in visitors and contractors?

The office of a New Zealand school signs in parent helpers, relief teachers, couriers and tradespeople, often through more than one gate.

Published 28 October 2026

I built SiteKey for New Zealand worksites, and schools, kura and tertiary campuses now have their own rate for it.

Who is responsible for visitors and contractors at a school?

The school board is responsible. The Ministry of Education’s guidance says boards are “legally responsible for the health and safety of everyone working at, attending and visiting a school”, and that a board is a person conducting a business or undertaking (PCBU) under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015. A university, polytechnic, wānanga or PTE is the PCBU for its own campuses.

The duty to visitors comes from section 36(2) of the Act. A PCBU “must ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, that the health and safety of other persons is not put at risk from work carried out as part of the conduct of the business or undertaking.”

A contractor’s business is a PCBU too, so on any job at the school two PCBUs have duties to the same people. Section 34(1) says that in that case each PCBU “must, so far as is reasonably practicable, consult, co-operate with, and co-ordinate activities with all other PCBUs who have a duty in relation to the same matter.” For property projects led by the New Zealand School Property Agency, contractors need a site specific safety plan, and an emergency plan agreed with the school.

What does a school have to check before a contractor starts?

The New Zealand School Property Agency says contractors and their employees “who may have unsupervised access to children during normal operating hours” must undergo a Police vet and risk assessment, under the Children’s Act 2014 and the Education and Training Act 2020. Contractors who will not have unsupervised access do not need a vet. The agency’s school access plan was developed to reduce the need for Police vetting, by setting out where contractors can work and how they stay apart from students.

Police vetting is the school’s process. The vet has to be kept confidential under the Privacy Act 2020 and the Public Records Act 2005, and a school cannot share it with another agency or accept one from another agency. SiteKey does not store Police vets and does not decide who is safe to be around students. A sign in system can record that a contractor read and accepted the access conditions, and when they arrived and left each day.

Why does the sign in record matter in an evacuation?

Every school must have fire evacuation procedures under the Fire and Emergency New Zealand (Fire Safety, Evacuation Procedures, and Evacuation Schemes) Regulations 2018, and enough staff trained to manage the evacuation of everyone on site. Class rolls account for students, and visitors and contractors are only on the roll call if someone recorded them coming in. A paper visitor book is only at the assembly point if a staff member carried it out of the office. A live list on a phone shows everyone signed in at every gate.

How long should a school keep visitor records?

Information privacy principle 9 of the Privacy Act 2020 says an agency “must not keep that information for longer than is required for the purposes for which the information may lawfully be used.” No set period applies to a sign in record. Decide how long you need sign in history for, write that into your privacy statement, and delete records once they reach it. SiteKey has a separate guide on how long to keep sign in records.

Common questions

Is a school board a PCBU?

Yes. The Ministry of Education says school boards are persons conducting a business or undertaking under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, responsible for the health and safety of workers, visitors and students at their school. A tertiary institute is the PCBU for its own campuses.

Do contractors working at a school need a Police vet?

Contractors and their employees who may have unsupervised access to students during normal school hours must undergo a Police vet and risk assessment. Contractors who will not have unsupervised access do not need one. A school access plan reduces the need for vetting by keeping contractors away from unsupervised access.

Does a school have to keep a visitor register?

No law names a visitor register as such. A board has duties to visitors and contractors under the Health and Safety at Work Act, and must have fire evacuation procedures. A current record of who is on the grounds is one way to show the school can account for everyone.

How long can a school keep visitor sign in records?

Information privacy principle 9 says personal information must not be kept for longer than is required for the purposes it may lawfully be used for. Set a retention period that matches why you collect the records, and delete them after it.
